WHO WE ARE

About ZoRent

ZoRent brings different types of accommodation and recreational places together in one platform.

Visitors can discover hotels, homestays, PGs, hostels, resorts, guest houses, picnic spots and camping sites across Mizoram.

Our goal is to make discovering places easier while giving local property owners a simple way to present their properties and connect directly with visitors.
